Popular Healthy Recipes

Simple, tasty, and nourishing recipes for your daily routine.

Fresh Garden Salad

A refreshing mix of leafy greens, tomatoes, cucumber, and a light lemon dressing.

⏱ 15 mins 🔥 ~180 kcal
Ingredients:
Lettuce, cucumber, tomato, carrots, lemon juice, olive oil, and pepper.

Fruit Oatmeal Bowl

Creamy oats topped with fresh fruit and nuts for a satisfying breakfast.

⏱ 10 mins 🔥 ~300 kcal
Ingredients:
Rolled oats, milk, banana, berries, almonds, and cinnamon.

Vegetable Lentil Bowl

A hearty, fiber-rich meal with lentils and colorful cooked vegetables.

⏱ 30 mins 🔥 ~350 kcal
Ingredients:
Cooked lentils, carrots, spinach, onion, tomato, garlic, and spices.

Balanced Power Bowl

A balanced combination of whole grains, vegetables, and plant-based protein.

⏱ 25 mins 🔥 ~400 kcal
Ingredients:
Brown rice, chickpeas, broccoli, bell pepper, olive oil, and herbs.

Fruit & Yogurt Bowl

A creamy snack with fresh fruit, yogurt, and crunchy seeds.

⏱ 5 mins 🔥 ~220 kcal
Ingredients:
Plain yogurt, banana, apple, chia seeds, and a few nuts.

Homemade Vegetable Soup

A comforting soup made with seasonal vegetables and aromatic herbs.

⏱ 25 mins 🔥 ~160 kcal
Ingredients:
Carrots, beans, peas, onion, tomato, vegetable stock, and herbs.

Healthy Eating Tips

Small everyday habits can support a healthier lifestyle.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day. Your fluid needs depend on activity, weather, and individual health.

🥦

Eat More Vegetables

Include a variety of colorful vegetables and fruits in your meals to get different nutrients.

🌾

Choose Whole Grains

Try oats, brown rice, whole-wheat roti, and other whole grains for fiber.

🥜

Include Protein

Add lentils, beans, chickpeas, eggs, dairy products, tofu, or nuts as appropriate.

🍽️

Eat Balanced Meals

Combine vegetables, protein-rich foods, whole grains, and healthy fats.

🧘

Eat Mindfully

Take your time while eating, notice hunger and fullness, and enjoy your meals.
